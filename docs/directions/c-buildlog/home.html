Burrow

Make time for what matters.

A habit time tracker. Tap an activity and your time starts, write a quick reflection when you finish, and set daily, weekly or monthly goals. Marko the marmot keeps you company.

HOW IT WORKSNo account. Burrow learns when you usually start, and nudges you only if you forget.
ON THE APP STOREIPHONE / OCT 2026

Engine Miles

Your odometer shows how far the car went.

A plug-in hybrid can run on electric for weeks, so the odometer says nothing about the gas engine. Engine Miles estimates engine miles from your fill-ups (gallons times gas-only MPG) and counts down to your next oil change.

HOW IT WORKSNo car connection and no account. It's an estimate, and your owner's manual still has the final say.

RelayReply

Phone calls, without the phone.

You type what you need, an AI makes the call and talks to the business, and you read both sides as live captions. When a price, a time or anything personal comes up, you get a card and decide.

THE SYSTEMA server places the real call, a voice model speaks, and a second model listens for questions only you can answer. Closing the tab doesn't end the call.
